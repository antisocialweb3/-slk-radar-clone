Working detection rules

Transparent, adjustable heuristics for the first version.

  • A-shape: a local peak relative to neighbouring candles; candidate zone is that pivot candle's open–close body.
  • V-shape: a local trough relative to neighbouring candles; candidate zone is that pivot candle's open–close body.
  • Open-Close: a comparatively large candle body versus recent bodies; its open–close body becomes a candidate zone.
  • Line mode: finds peaks/troughs using closing prices, then marks the pivot candle's body zone.
  • Candidate proximity is measured against the latest returned candle close. Historical zones are not proof of a future rejection.

Important limitations

  • These are provisional computational interpretations. They may not match every nuance of your personal SLK examples yet.
  • The feed refreshes only when you load/refresh it; this is not a tick-by-tick streaming feed.
  • No buy/sell calls, stop-losses, targets, or guaranteed rejections are generated.
  • Do not use the output for live trading until the detected zones have been compared against your chart examples and back-tested.

SLK Method Rulebook

Rules captured from your method so far. Detection remains provisional until the remaining definitions are confirmed.

RULES IN PROGRESS

1 · Bias building

  • Weekly: study candle rejection and where the candle closes.
  • Daily: look for a sweep-and-break setup or an external breakout. Either can qualify independently.
  • 4H: apply the same sweep-and-break or external-breakout rules. A breakout on the second day after a Daily rejection adds validation, but is not mandatory.
  • Break confirmation: candle body must close beyond the relevant external high or low. Wick-only breaks and internal breakouts do not qualify.

2 · Setup confluence

  • Find an unfilled three-candle imbalance (FVG).
  • An A-shape, V-shape, or Open-Close key level must overlap inside the imbalance.
  • Liquidity must be close to the imbalance. A single candle can count as liquidity, using its whole high-to-low range.
  • A reaction from the overlapping key level can be a rejection candle or a strong move away.
  • The reaction must then be confirmed by a body-close break of the relevant external high or low.
Important: the current chart detector still uses simple candidate heuristics. This rulebook records your method; it does not yet mean every rule is automatically detected or validated by the scanner.
